Bangkok

Bangkok, the vibrant capital city of Thailand, is a bustling metropolis known for its rich culture, ornate temples, lively street markets, and exciting nightlife. From the majestic Grand Palace and Wat Arun to the bustling streets of Khao San Road, Bangkok offers a unique blend of traditional and modern attractions that captivate every traveler.

Must See

You shouldn't miss these must-see attractions in Bangkok:

  • The Grand Palace: A stunning complex of buildings that served as the official residence of the Kings of Siam.
  • Wat Pho: Home to the famous Reclining Buddha statue and a center for traditional Thai massage.
  • Floating Market: Experience the vibrant atmosphere of traditional floating markets where vendors sell fresh produce and local delicacies from boats.
  • Tichuca Rooftop Bar: Enjoy panoramic views of the city skyline while sipping on a refreshing cocktail.

    • Tutor Tips

    Thailand Travel Tutor tips to help you plan and create your own memories in Thailand.

    It is very hot and humid in Bangkok, so be sure to stay hydrated and wear light, breathable clothing. This city has alot of pollution, so consider wearing a mask if you have respiratory issues. Use public transportation like the Skytrain or tuktuks to navigate the city efficiently and avoid traffic congestion. Don't forget to try local street food, but ensure it's from reputable vendors to avoid any stomach issues. Lastly, be respectful of local customs and dress modestly when visiting temples.
