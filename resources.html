Travel Resources

Here are some useful resources to help you plan your trip to Thailand:

  • How to fly cheaply to Thailand: Search Google Flights and set alerts for your desired destination so that when the flights are below average pricing you are given an email alert
  • Credit Card Points: Alternatively if you have a good credit score look into different credit cards that offer points that you can use to book your flight, such as, American Express, Chase Sapphire, or Capital One Venture (many of these card have an intro offer where you can earn up to 90,000 points if you spend $4,000 in the first 3 months, you just have to look around for a good offer) You can use a site like The Points Guy to compare cards and see which one is best for you.
  • Points Value: you can find good value using Points Yeah Just sign up for a free account, then compare where to transfer your points for the best redemption value.
  • Booking accommodation: Make sure you shop around because with a a trip like Thailand you will be staying in various cities, you might find a better deal with a different platform for each leg of your jouney. I recommend checking through Airbnb or Booking.com or Expedia for a wide range of options from budget to luxury stays.
  • Travel Insurance: Consider purchasing travel insurance to protect yourself against unexpected events such as trip cancellations, medical emergencies, or lost luggage. Make sure to check all options for the best price and read the policy details carefully.
  • AI to help plan your itinerary: Consider using AI tools like ChatGPT to help you plan your daily activities, find hidden gems, and optimize your travel route.
    • Example Prompt for 3 days in Bangkok: "Create a 3-day Bangkok itinerary with temples, markets, viewpoints, nightlife, and food. Fast-paced schedule, no long breaks. Morning/afternoon/evening sections. Include travel times, restaurant suggestions, estimated budget, and tips. Organized with Day headings + bullet points
    • You can add more customization to the prompt to suit your preferences and interests by specifying the pace you want to travel, your budget preferences, types of activities you enjoy, and any specific landmarks or experiences you want to include.
